## Forum

I'm looking for 15 to 25 Seeker owners to test Veto's first public Android APK.
Veto lets you approve rules for an agent's payments and trades with your wallet,
then read what was paid or refused and why. Payment decisions can be exported.

The trade demo shows an agent key trying to send output elsewhere, use another
pool or exceed the approved limits. The program records the refusals. This is
a scripted demonstration; the phone does not run an agent for you.

There is also Hold: a vault where withdrawals to new addresses wait, and a
separate guardian key can stop a waiting withdrawal or freeze the vault.
Testing that flow needs a guardian key and an explicit safe address.

This release is devnet only. It uses test money with no value, not real funds.
You'll need a Seeker with a devnet wallet, free devnet SOL for fees and free
devnet USDC. Other Android phones with a compatible Solana wallet can also
follow the guide.

I'd like feedback on setup, reviewing a rule, understanding a refusal and
stopping a held withdrawal. If you cannot finish a step, that is useful feedback
too. Please use the tester issue form in the guide and describe where you got
stuck. Do not post private keys or recovery phrases.

Try: https://veto-hq.github.io

Setup, known limits and feedback: [docs/TESTERS.md](TESTERS.md)
