How grades work Devnet

Four grades, plain rules.

A request is a payment your agent asked for: the rule either let it through or refused it. The grade is the share of requests that were outside the rule.

Stayed inside its rule

Fewer than 1 request in 20 outside its rule.

Tested its limit now and then

1 to 4 requests in 20 outside its rule.

Often asked outside its rule

More than 4 requests in 20 outside its rule.

Too new to grade

Fewer than 10 requests, or fewer than 3 days running. The facts still show; the label waits.

What counts, and what does not
Outside its rule means the rule refused it: over your limit, the wrong payee, or outside its dates. Each refusal is saved with its reason.
A payment you allowed once still counts as asking outside: the rule refused it first. If you had to allow 2 or more, the grade shows one step lower.
Not counted: the agent's own signed declines, and money moved outside the rule. That is always 0 because the rule makes it impossible, so it is no credit to the agent.

Every count is a fact saved on the blockchain, and anyone with the rule's address can check it. A grade describes how the agent behaved at its limits. It is not a safety guarantee.
