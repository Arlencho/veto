Hold: big money waits, and a second key can say no.

The promise: if someone gets your key, a big withdrawal or one to a new address has to wait, and you or your guardian can stop it before it goes through. Anything large waits 1, 2 or 3 days on the blockchain clock, and you or your second key can stop it, freeze the vault, or move everything to a safe address you chose while calm.

Backglass direction Same program, same Seed Vault, same public record Tightening is instant. Loosening waits.

1. How it works

Two doors out of one vault.

You move money into a Hold vault, an account the Veto program controls, so every withdrawal must pass a rule. Sample: 1,000 test USDC.

Everyday door: instant Up to 50 per day (your limit) to addresses you have paid before. No wait.
Big door: held Anything else waits 2 days (your choice). Held, not refused. It goes if nobody stops it.
While it waits You or your guardian key can Stop that withdrawal, Freeze the vault, or Recover everything to your safe address. Each stop is saved on the blockchain, like a refusal today.

The wait runs on the blockchain clock. The program will not pay before it has passed, whether or not your phone was awake.

2. What triggers a hold

Small and familiar goes now. Big, new or looser waits.

  • Over your everyday limit. More than 50 in one day, on its own or added up.
  • To a new address. The first payment to any address this vault has never paid, whatever the amount.
  • A big share of the vault. More than 25% of what it holds within 24 hours, even to a known address.
  • Anything that loosens the rules. A higher limit, a shorter wait, removing the guardian key, a new safe address: each waits the full delay, so a thief cannot switch the protection off first.

Tightening (a lower limit, a longer wait, adding a guardian) is instant. Sample: 1,000 to unknown 8xQf...Tz9A trips three of the four. Held for 2 days.

3. The wait you choose

Picked once, changed slowly.

1dayMoney you touch often
2daysSuggested. A weekend away still fits
3daysSavings you rarely move
  • Shortening the wait is itself held for the current wait. Lengthening it is instant.
  • Skipping the wait for one withdrawal needs both keys, signed separately. One key can never skip.
  • Stopping needs one key and no wait. Saying no is always the fastest thing you can do.

The countdown on screen reads the blockchain clock. The app can be late or asleep; the wait cannot.

4. Who can do what

Your key drives. The guardian only brakes.

A stolen guardian key gains a thief nothing. Without a wait, a stolen owner key gains at most the everyday amount, and only to addresses the vault has paid before.

Alerts come from the cloud watcher and from the app's own check about every 15 minutes. Miss every one and the wait still runs its full length. Safety never depends on seeing a notification in time.

6. Fits Veto, built for Seeker

Same rule engine. The agent is you, on a bad day.

  • Rules, not trust. Today an agent asks and the program decides. Hold does the same for a withdrawal from your own vault.
  • Every stop is on the record. Held, stopped, frozen, recovered, released: each saved on the blockchain with who and why, next to today's refusals.
  • Seed Vault signs everything. Your key and the guardian key both live in Seed Vault; each action is a fingerprint or PIN. Keys never leave the phone.
  • Two homes for the guardian key. A second key in Seed Vault on the same phone (quick; stops a stolen hot key or a scam signed by mistake), or your second Seeker (also covers a stolen seed phrase). The app suggests the second phone when it sees one.

The safe address is picked at setup, ideally your second Seeker. Recovery to it is instant because you chose it while calm; changing it waits.

7. Honest limits

What Hold does not do.

  • Only money inside the vault is protected. Your normal wallet stays as fast and as exposed as before; nothing on Solana can pause it.
  • A thief with your key still gets the everyday amount, here 50 a day, until you freeze. Keep that limit small.
  • A guardian key on the same phone and seed does not stop someone who has the seed phrase. For that, the guardian must be the second Seeker.
  • With no guardian, you and a thief sharing your key can cancel each other forever: money locked, not stolen. The guardian key ends that fight.
  • Lose both keys and nobody, Veto included, can reach the money. Veto cannot reach in to help you, so it cannot help a thief either.
  • Devnet and test tokens today. Works for SOL, USDC and any token you move in; no special token needed.

8. Why this beats a normal wallet

A normal wallet has no undo. This one has a no.

  • Time is the defence, not secrecy. Hardware wallets guard the key. Hold guards the decision: even a signed withdrawal must survive two days in public.
  • A brake key, not a co-owner. Multi-signature wallets need two people for every payment. Here everyday money stays instant and the second key can only say no.
  • Protection cannot be switched off quickly. Every loosening waits as long as a withdrawal would. The first thing a thief wants is the slowest.
  • A pre-decided escape. Recovery to the safe address works with one key in one tap, because you chose the destination in advance.
  • A public track record. "Veto stopped 1,000 from leaving" is a fact anyone can verify on the blockchain, not a claim.

9. The sample case, start to finish

1,000 test USDC to 8xQf...Tz9A, never paid before.

  1. Vault holds 1,000. Everyday limit 50 a day. Wait 2 days. Guardian key on your second Seeker.
  2. A request: 1,000 to 8xQf...Tz9A. Over 50, new address, all of the vault. Held. Held
  3. Both phones: "Held: 1,000 to a new address. Goes Sunday 14:12 unless you stop it."
  4. It was you? Wait, or sign on both phones to let it go now.
  5. Not you? Tap Stop on either phone (one fingerprint). Then Freeze: not even the everyday 50 can leave. Frozen
  6. Tap Recover from the guardian phone: all 1,000 moves to your safe address at once. The thief holds a key to an empty room.
  7. The record reads: held, stopped by guardian key, frozen, recovered. On the blockchain with times.
